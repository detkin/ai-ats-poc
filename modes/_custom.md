# `_custom.md` — house rules

Tenant layer (`DATA_CONTRACT.md` §1): yours, never rewritten by an engine update. This is where
a people team writes the conventions that are not policy values — the things `tenant/policy.yml`
has no key for, but that you want the operator to follow every run.

**This file ships empty on purpose.** No rules are in force below.

Scope, so a house rule cannot quietly become a safety hole:

- House rules may **narrow** behaviour: fewer nudges, quieter windows, extra wording, an extra
  human in the loop, a named person to route a class of escalation to.
- House rules may **not widen** it. Nothing here can authorise a write, skip a policy check,
  turn a proposal into a decision, or override anything in `modes/_shared.md`. If a rule here
  and a rule in `_shared.md` disagree, `_shared.md` wins and you say so in your summary.
- Keep each rule to a sentence or two, and say _why_. A rule nobody can explain is a rule that
  gets applied wrongly.
